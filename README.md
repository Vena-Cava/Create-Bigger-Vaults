Create: Bigger Vaults expands on the Create mod's storage by adding new tiers of Item Vaults, Fluid Tanks and Steam Engines.

Each tier offers different storage capacities while remaining fully compatible with Create's existing mechanics.

The Wooden tier offers a smaller but cheaper alternative, allowing you to get started with large storage systems much earlier in your playthrough.

## Features

### Item Vaults

Item Vaults are available in the following material tiers:

- Wooden  - 0.5x Capacity - Max Length: 6
- Copper - 1.5x Capacity - Max Length: 9
- Iron - 2.0x Capacity - Max Length: 12
- Emerald - 2.5x Capacity - Max Length: 12
- Gold - 3.0x Capacity - Max Length: 15
- Diamond - 3.5x Capacity - Max Length: 18
- Obsidian - 4.0x Capacity - Max Length: 18
- Netherite - 5.0x Capacity - Max Length: 21

Capacity increases with every tier. Certain tiers also increase the maximum length of the vault, allowing for much larger storage systems as your factory grows.
The Max Length displayed above is based on a 3×3 cross-section.

### Fluid Tanks

Fluid Tanks follow their own progression:

- Wooden - 0.5x
- Zinc - 1.5x
- Iron - 2.0x
- Emerald - 2.5x
- Gold - 3.0x
- Diamond - 3.5x
- Obsidian - 4.0x
- Netherite - 5.0x

Each tier increases the amount of fluid stored per tank block, allowing significantly larger capacities while keeping the same maximum multiblock size.

Fluid Tanks include support for Steam Boilers, which produce increasing amounts of Stress Units.

### Steam Engines

Steam Engines are available in the same material tiers as Fluid Tanks.

Higher tier Steam Engines have a greater Stress Capacity than lower tiers, allowing you to have the same stress output using a lower amount of Steam Engines.

## Configuration

Material Tier multipliers can be configured to better suit different modpacks and playstyles.
